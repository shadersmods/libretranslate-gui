# Code of Conduct

People with different backgrounds and levels of experience are welcome here. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## How to participate

- Be respectful. Explain disagreements with code, evidence, or examples.
- Give useful feedback and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Respect people's names, identities, privacy, and time.
- Keep discussions relevant and help newcomers find the information they need.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual attention or content directed at participants, and publishing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are repeated personal attacks, spam, or continued disruption after a maintainer asks you to stop.

## Reporting a problem

Email [neo@shadersmods.com](mailto:neo@shadersmods.com) with a description of what happened and links or screenshots, if available. Include any immediate safety or privacy concerns. You do not need to confront the person involved or post a public report.

Reports will be handled privately. Information will be shared only with people who need it to assess the report or take action, or when disclosure is legally required. Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern or helping with a report is itself a violation.

For software vulnerabilities, use the reporting process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and limit participation to protect project spaces. Depending on the behavior and its impact, this may mean a request to stop, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment may lead to an immediate ban.

Enforcement applies to maintainers and contributors alike. Decisions should consider the available evidence, the impact on affected people, and any repeated behavior. Anyone affected by a decision may email the address above with additional context or a request for reconsideration.
